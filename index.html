Do you provide cargo insurance?
↓

Yes. While we exercise the utmost care with all shipments, we highly recommend comprehensive marine or inland transit insurance. SGC provides direct Marine & Cargo Insurance coverage to give you total peace of mind.

What is your liability limit for damaged goods?
↓

Our liability is strictly governed by the terms and conditions outlined in our standard Bill of Lading and Waybill, typically based on a per-kilogram rate for uninsured cargo. Please refer to our Service Agreement for exact details.

How do you handle hazardous materials (Hazmat)?
↓

We handle select classes of hazardous materials subject to prior approval. Shippers must provide the complete Material Safety Data Sheet (MSDS) and ensure all packaging complies with local and international regulations. Special handling surcharges will apply.

Our Complete Story

SOTELO GROUP OF COMPANIES

Founded in 1999, the Sotelo Group of Companies (SGC) was established with a singular vision: to unify fragmented logistics operations across the Philippines and provide a seamless, end-to-end supply chain solution.

Over the decades, we have evolved from a traditional freight forwarder into a formidable logistics conglomerate comprising three specialized subsidiaries:

  • Philippine Nationwide Logistics Inc. (PNLI): Our core freight brokerage and forwarding arm, ensuring smooth customs clearance and cargo movement.
  • Trans-Visayas Warehousing & Logistics Inc. (TVWLI): Managing our massive, reliable fleet of trucks designed for domestic overland transport.
  • Gateway Container Line Philippines, Inc. (GCLPI): Registered as an NVOCC (Non-Vessel Operating Common Carrier), Breakbulk Agent, and Cargo Consolidator managing International Air and Sea Freight.

Today, powered by ISO 9001:2015 and ISO 45001:2018 certifications, our continuously trained workforce operates 24/7. We don't just move cargo; we move trust, serving as the logistics backbone for industries ranging from manufacturing to aerospace.

Incoterms Quick Guide

A quick reference for the most common international shipping terms used by SGC.

EXW (Ex Works)

The buyer assumes all risks and costs from the seller's door to the final destination.

FOB (Free on Board)

The seller covers costs to load the goods onto the vessel. Buyer takes over from the port of origin.

CIF (Cost, Insurance & Freight)

The seller covers the freight and insurance to deliver goods to the buyer's port of destination.

DDP (Delivered Duty Paid)

The seller assumes all costs, risks, duties, and taxes to deliver the goods to the buyer's final door.

Unsure which term to use for your next shipment? Ask our team.
